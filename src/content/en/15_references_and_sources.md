# References and Sources

## Categorization of Sources in This Book

This book treats the following four categories distinctly:

1.  **Jung's Original Texts**: Discourses on *Psychological Types*, one-sidedness, compensation, inferior functions, and individuation.
2.  **Official Myers-Briggs Theory and Ethics**: The four dichotomies, best-fit type, type development, use of non-preferred functions, and ethical principles such as not using the instrument for hiring or selection.
3.  **Empirical Research**: Studies on MBTI psychometrics, labels and fixed personality views, plasticity of personality traits, implementation intentions, and perspective-taking.
4.  **Practices Proposed in This Book**: The Four-Step Choice Practice ("Notice / Reconsider / Try Small / Reweave") and the four pairs of bidirectional exercises.

The fourth category is neither a method proposed by Jung nor a psychotherapy that has verified its effectiveness in its original form. It is a practice proposal for general readers, combining the direction derived from Jung's "noticing one-sidedness and opposites" with small elements derived from modern research on behavioral change.

## A Request to the Reader

Research updates, and evaluations of scales and theories also change. The explanations in this book are based on the above materials and strive to separate the strength and limitations of evidence as of the time of publication.

The value of the four-letter code is not determined by whether it is entirely correct or entirely incorrect.

Does that phrase increase the questions you ask yourself and others to see them better? Or does it decide the answer before you look?

The sources are provided so that readers can verify these distinctions themselves.
